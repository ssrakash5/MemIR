Migrate user-based service accounts to workload identities

Some organizations use user accounts in Microsoft Entra ID as service accounts for
automation. With mandatory MFA enforcement, it's critical to migrate these user-based
service accounts to secure cloud-based service accounts with workload identities.
Workload identities, including managed identities and service principals, are designed
for automation scenarios and don't require MFA. These identities provide a more secure
and manageable solution.

Use role-based access control

Access management for cloud resources is critical for any organization that uses the
cloud. Azure role-based access control (Azure RBAC) helps you manage who has access to
Azure resources, what they can do with those resources, and which areas they can access.
