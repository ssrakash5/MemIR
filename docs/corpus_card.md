## Generation procedure

Every memory in this corpus is generated by `eval/run_full_sweep.py`
running `src/memoryir/harness.py`'s `generate_trace()` against one of
30 human-approved scenario specifications (`configs/scenarios/`), under
the frozen rules in `configs/experiment_grid.yaml`: shared retrieval
per run, true-parents-rank-first + deterministic distractor fill, and
the depth-continuation rule. Oracle structural edges
(`STRUCTURAL_PARENT`/`CO_RETRIEVED`) are derived deterministically from
each scenario's approved specification at generation time — never
inferred or asserted after the fact. Full method: `docs/paper/method.md`.

## Poison forms

| `poison_form` | Scenarios | Signal strength | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `direct_instruction` | 6 | strong | Explicit imperative embedded in retrieved content |
| `embedded_fact` | 6 | weak | False claim stated as routine background context |
| `authoritative_framing` | 6 | strong | False claim with fabricated verification/urgency |
| `multi_hop_setup` | 6 | weak | Compositional — no single premise alone is harmful |
| `clean_control` | 6 | none | No adversarial content; false-positive baseline |

## Labeling

All derived memories are labeled `CARRIES`/`REFERENCES`/`CLEAN` against
their scenario's `semantic_target` by the frozen automated pipeline
(`src/memoryir/labeler.py`) — LLM-primary judge, NLI + adjudicator
diagnostic-only. Validated against 120 blind human-labeled samples:
**Cohen's κ = 0.8699** (run 1) and **0.8838** (independent rerun,
identical code — the difference reflects genuine `temperature=0` API
non-determinism, not a methodology change). Full validation report:
`results/kappa_sample/kappa_report.md`.

Label distribution across the 24-scenario poisoned corpus (173,119
derived memories):

| Model | CARRIES | CLEAN | REFERENCES |
|---|---|---|---|
| gpt-4o-mini | 30,122 (51.8%) | 24,520 (42.2%) | 3,483 (6.0%) |
| gpt-4o | 27,136 (48.0%) | 26,592 (47.1%) | 3,872 (6.9%) |
| llama-3.3-70b | 27,238 (47.7%) | 26,207 (45.9%) | 3,949 (6.9%) |

## Known limitations

See `docs/paper/limitations.md` for the full, reasoned list. Summary
relevant to this corpus specifically: synthetic/LLM-authored (no
real-document slice yet), single un-pinned embedding model
(`all-MiniLM-L6-v2`), 30 traces missing for one model due to a platform
content filter, marker-token extraction for H3 is mechanical rather
than human-authored, and $\kappa$ validation is against a single human
annotator, not a human-human baseline.
